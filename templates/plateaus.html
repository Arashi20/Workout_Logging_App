ℹ️ How Plateau Detection Works

For each exercise, the last 6 sessions are split into an early half and a recent half. Both your estimated 1RM (strength) and session volume are compared across the two halves. If neither metric improved by more than 2.5%, the exercise is flagged as a plateau. If only one metric stagnated it is flagged as stagnating. At least 4 logged sessions are required per exercise.
